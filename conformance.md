# Conformance status

**Draft 0.2.** The user deferred the testing regime until the contract is written.

Normative requirements are in [contract.md](contract.md), [publication-contract.md](publication-contract.md), [accepted-offer.md](accepted-offer.md) and [protocol-architecture.md](protocol-architecture.md). The current schema and example checker provide only authoring feedback on record structure, content references and selected consistency constraints. They do not implement or qualify a marketplace.

There is no adopted runtime conformance suite, evaluator, human-review procedure, correction policy, payment qualification gate or performance benchmark. The previous 63-case plan belongs to [archived 0.1](archive/0.1/conformance.md), which has different semantics and must not be treated as the current acceptance suite.

After contract review, a test regime can exercise independent participants, admission and revision behavior, exact adoption, notification/refusal races and downstream consumer interpretation. An internal toy consumer may simulate payments outside the protocol. These are future design activities, not work performed or criteria already selected.
